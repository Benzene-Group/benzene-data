What the Gates mean:
Gate 2

A reserved seat. Confirmed connection date and point, and a secure place in the reordered queue.

Gate 1

A standby ticket. Indicative date and location only. Can apply for Gate 2 later.

No Gate shown

The register's Gate field is blank, so it does not say which.

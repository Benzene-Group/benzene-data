# Benzene

> UK grid connection intelligence from primary public sources. Benzene tells developers, land and planning advisers, lawyers and investors whether a site can connect to the grid and when, using NESO's connection register and the planning and contract records around it. Every figure is labelled confirmed, reported or inferred.

## What Benzene does

- A free interactive map of Britain's transmission connection queue: https://www.benzene-group.com/queue-map.html
- A free weekly briefing on what changed in the underlying data, with sources
- Fixed-fee screening reports on a specific site or project (£1,950 fixed, one site, 5 working days), checking the connection queue, the wait, competing projects, and the planning and revenue-contract records

Research is produced with AI systems that read primary sources directly, and every report is reviewed before it is sent. Benzene reports what public records say. It does not provide engineering, legal or investment advice.

## Gate 1 and Gate 2

Under NESO's connections reform, Gate 2 projects receive a confirmed connection date, a confirmed connection point and a secure place in the reordered queue. Gate 1 projects receive only an indicative date and location. Source: https://www.neso.energy/news/connections-reform-key-unlocking-gb-growth

## Status tags

- Confirmed: read directly from a named primary source
- Reported: from a named secondary source, not yet confirmed at source
- Inferred: a deduction from confirmed data, with the reasoning shown

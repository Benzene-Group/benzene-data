From the briefing

Three recent signals, and why they matter

A sample of the free weekly briefing. Every figure carries its source and a status tag.

Connections reform
31 Oct

On 25 September 2026 NESO asked Ofgem to move the Gate 2 Phase 1 offer deadline from 30 September to 31 October 2026. A further extension has been reported but we have not found it confirmed.

So what: firm offers are arriving later than planned, so dates built on them may slip.

Balancing costs, Sept 2026
£541m

NESO's balancing costs for September 2026, of which about £390m was paying for grid constraints. NESO's own month-ahead forecast was £306m.

So what: most of that money pays for a shortage of network capacity, the same shortage that holds up new connections.
